# E1 v5.27: Multiobjective continuous summer event comparison

Source: successful v5.25 3-independent-GCM archive (run 45948547935). Uses each GCM's own simulated 2050 summer SSP245/585 regional native daily mean TAS, 91 continuous target days and original 54 Historical-only frozen neural checkpoints. No raw climate field reads or future tuning.

Within EACH GCM×SSP cell, compare Persistence with six pre-existing neural training arms (FNO/U-Net × Historical hot training weight 1×/2×/4×) using three descriptive mean metrics: weighted RMSE (minimize), ≥3 consecutive same-pixel Historical-month-local P95 hot-event-day Recall (maximize), and Precision (maximize). A point is Pareto dominated if another is no worse in every objective and strictly better in at least one. Output all 42 candidates, each cell's frontier and GCM-level descriptive counts. No conflation of SSPs with independent climate ensembles and no model choice based on this future evaluation. A candidate that is non-dominated has NOT been prospectively proven better. The next scientific priority is blinded multi-year evaluation of originally fixed candidate configurations and evaluation of heat-event duration distribution and censoring. The data are future simulated daily mean TAS, not observed tasmax.
